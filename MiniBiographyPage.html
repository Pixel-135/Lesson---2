The Person I Admire The Most..

Lara from KATSEYE

Full name - Lara Raj (Lara Rajagopalan).

Identity: She is an American singer, dancer, and actress of Indian and Sri Lankan Tamil descent.

Language: She was raised in the US but speaks Tamil fluently.

• Fashion & Identity: She is known for mixing modern streetwear with traditional elements like bindis and nose rings, using her global platform to loudly celebrate South Asian culture.

• Big Achievement: She made history as a member of KATSEYE, which was formed through the global talent show The Debut: Dream Academy.

Lara Raj is a trailblazing American singer, dancer, actress, and songwriter of Indian and Sri Lankan Tamil descent who has achieved massive international superstardom as a core member of the global girl group KATSEYE. Born in Stamford, Connecticut, and raised in Texas and New York within a highly musical family environment, Lara trained extensively in classical music, musical theater, and dance from an early age, even appearing on television and hosting events before her music career took off. She skyrocketed to prominence on the reality survival show The Debut: Dream Academy—a joint venture between entertainment giants HYBE and Geffen Records—where her exceptional vocal range, fierce artistic confidence, magnetic stage presence, and fluent Tamil heritage immediately captivated a global fanbase. Making history as one of the first South Asian artists to debut under a major global music training system, Lara has shattered cultural boundaries in the mainstream pop industry. Since KATSEYE's explosive debut with their hit EP SIS (Soft Is Strong), Lara’s career has reached astronomical heights, cementing her status as an industry powerhouse. She achieved a major creative milestone by becoming the first member of KATSEYE to secure official songwriting credits after co-writing the hit track "Unloveu"—a song she originally began crafting when she was just sixteen years old. Her individual talent has shone so brightly that she was announced for a historic solo performance spot at the prestigious Weverse Con Festival. With KATSEYE, Lara has dominated the global music charts and major awards circuits, earning official Grammy nominations for Best New Artist and Best Pop Duo/Group Performance for their track "Gabriela," alongside winning Push Performance of the Year at the MTV Video Music Awards (VMAs) for their viral sensation "Touch." The group also swept the American Music Awards (AMAs), taking home New Artist of the Year and Breakthrough Pop Artist, a monumental night where Lara proudly highlighted her roots by concluding their global acceptance speech in her native language with the phrase "Romba romba nandri." Beyond her musical and performance accolades, Lara is a vital cultural icon and a fierce advocate for identity and representation. After publicly coming out as queer to her fans on the Weverse app, she was honored with the Blooming Impact Award from the Los Angeles LGBT Center and named one of GLAAD's most influential LGBTQ+ youth. By seamlessly blending high-end modern streetwear with traditional South Asian elements like bindis and nose rings on international red carpets, Lara constantly uses her massive global platform to celebrate her dual heritage. Her relentless work ethic, unapologetic authenticity, and dedication to breaking barriers inspire young creators worldwide to confidently embrace their uniqueness, take pride in their roots, and chase their biggest dreams on the world stage.
